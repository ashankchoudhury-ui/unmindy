## First Comment (write this yourself on launch day)
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Ashank, a 15-year-old student from India. I built UNMINDY because I kept losing hours to doomscrolling without realizing it was happening.

The problem isn't that we don't know we should stop scrolling. It's that we don't realize we're scrolling until 45 minutes in.

Existing app blockers don't work — I just unlock them. The issue is awareness, not access.

UNMINDY detects the 3-second autopilot switch (the moment your brain goes from conscious to autopilot), interrupts with one question: "What are you avoiding?", then locks you into that task.

It's not a blocker. It's a pattern interrupt.

Currently in waitlist phase. Would love your feedback on the concept and the landing page.

What's your biggest scroll trigger? For me it's opening Instagram "to check one notification" and losing an hour.
